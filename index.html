A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Shubham.

Designer. Camera carrier. Long-drive enthusiast.

I’m a Senior UX Designer at Philips India, with two postgraduate qualifications in design and over six years of experience. My family has roots in New Delhi, and I live in Bengaluru with my mother and younger brother.

I enjoy travelling, photography, good food, and long drives with old-school music. A FRIENDS rerun is always welcome, too. I’m easygoing, a little playful, and happiest with life’s simple pleasures. I value trust, honesty, loyalty, and the ability to talk things through with kindness.

I’m hoping to meet a grounded, caring person who can be both a friend and a partner—someone who values close relationships and has dreams of her own. I’d love to build a life where we support each other, share the everyday, and find plenty of reasons to laugh.
